Inviscid Flow Over an Airfoil

A closed-form potential-flow solution — freestream flow around a circle, conformally mapped onto a Joukowski airfoil (z = ζ + b²/ζ), with circulation fixed by the Kutta condition at the trailing edge. The angle of attack and airfoil shape set the flow pattern; freestream speed sets how fast it moves.

This is an inviscid, irrotational solution with no boundary layer, so it can't predict stall or separation — lift keeps climbing with angle of attack here in a way a real, viscous flow wouldn't much past roughly 12–16°. It's also why the streamline pattern only depends on angle of attack and airfoil shape: doubling the freestream speed doubles every velocity in the field but leaves the pattern itself unchanged.
